# Windowing vs weights at target-pair carriers, unselected (#146)

Generated by `python -m training.carrier_windowing` (from `tools/`); do not edit by hand. The machine-readable report is [`carrier-windowing.json`](carrier-windowing.json).

> **Against the mushaf, not truth.** Every rate compares a decode with the realized reference. Nothing here was listened to, no threshold is chosen and no protocol is recommended: it measures how much of a swap rate moves with the window protocol and how much with the weights, on carriers no model outcome selected.

| carriers | the first of each run of a target-pair letter (a geminate is one carrier); a `ذ`, `ظ`, `س`, `ط` or `ق` carrier counts in both its pairs. In-word geminates: the first of a doubled consonant whose run stays inside one word |
| outcome | what the #84 scorer reads at the carrier (`training.site_outcomes`, the `contrast_attribution.aligned_consonants` alignment): **swap** = the partner letter (a geminate: one consonant), **mushaf** = the mushaf's letter (geminate held), **other** = anything else, another letter or nothing aligned |
| weights | 1 / the clip's inclusion probability (`mining_pool/clips.jsonl`) |
| intervals | acceptance rules §1: reciter-clustered bootstrap, B = 10,000, seed 20261008, type-7 percentiles. A cell too sparse or degenerate for the bootstrap shows **W** (Wilson, only for independent equally weighted carriers) or **(n/c)** (no admissible bound) |
| gaps | paired on the same carriers: both swap rates recomputed in every replicate of one reciter draw. **Windowing** = stream − whole per model; **weights** = `h448` − base per protocol |

A rate is `percent [95% interval] (unweighted carriers with that outcome)`; a gap is in points. `*` marks a row with fewer than 10 reciters or 20 carriers: it cannot support a claim.

## Population

- **Pool.** The #83 mining pool: 2,508 clips of 394 reciters, drawn from shards `h448` never trained on.
- **Cache.** The #85 haraka-gap cache streams the 2,084 clips (381 reciters, 3,060 kept segments) that ADR-0007's window build accepts. Left out with their reasons: `dropped_segment` 137, `over_long` 29, `phonetizer_unsupported` 69, `re_read` 163, `repeated_recitation` 25, `target_too_long` 1. Those clips have no stream decode, so nothing here covers re-read, partly dropped, unphonetizable or over-long recitations.
- **`h448`-unseen.** Of the cached clips, 337 hold a recording tied, as itself, an alias or a probable copy, to `h448`'s training shards, `decode_evalset` or the sealed panel (`tadabur.power_frame.unseen_clips`, Ship A's rule). They are left out of the main tables: a weights comparison on audio one model was trained or selected on is not one. That leaves **1,747 clips, 374 reciters, 2,668 kept segments**; 871 of the clips have inclusion probability 1. The whole cache is reported as a sensitivity below.
- **Carriers.** Every target-pair carrier and in-word geminate of those kept segments, from the realized reference alone: no carrier is chosen by a decode.
- **Weights.** 1 / the clip's inclusion probability: the pool over-samples clips where the base teacher swapped a pair or collapsed a geminate in the census of the drawn reciters, so inference is to those reciters' frame, not to all Tadabur reciters.

**The stream's reference.** A stream decode covers the clip's whole recitation span; it is read against the clip's kept-segment references joined by one space (`' '`), the word separator they already use, so two segments' letters never merge into one run or one normalized group. Every cached clip keeps all its segments, so no recited word is missing from that reference. The alignment is one local Smith-Waterman over up to 40 s: a carrier outside its span, and audio past the last full window (never decoded), commit nothing and count as `other`.
