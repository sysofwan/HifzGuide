## How a flag is classified

Each consonant error the app's kept grade records for a false flag (another consonant, a target or soft pair, a collapsed geminate) is placed on Muraja's reference for its ayah (`quran.db`: the phonetizer over the whole ayah, wasl inside, waqf at its end) at the group the word scorer numbers, and read in order:

1. **reference, the phonetizer:** the mushaf's marks say the letter is not pronounced as itself there (a silent letter, a hamzat al-wasl inside the ayah, an unmarked letter before a shaddah, an unmarked noon, an unmarked meem before baa, a tanween marked as not izhar or for iqlab) and the phonetizer kept it;
2. **reference, Muraja's word scorer:** what was recited is known (QUD, Tadabur: the item's realized reference, its last word in waqf) and does not hold the letter there;
3. **unclear:** what was recited is not known (device sessions) and the letter drops if the reader paused on the word, started at it, or joined it across an ayah boundary;
4. otherwise Hafs pronounces the letter. **Grading** (Muraja's word scorer) where the committed decode holds it there and the engine flagged it anyway; **model** where the decode has another letter or nothing there. The owner's sessions have no decode in the repo: the owner's verdict stands in where the phone showed the word, else the kept grade alone.

A flag whose letter is not in the word, or whose candidate letters (no group kept) are classified differently, is **unclear**. Idgham, iqlab, lam shamsiyyah and the madd and hamzat al-wasl dropped in wasl are already applied in Muraja's reference (`مِّن لَّدُنۡهُ` is `ممممِ للَدُنهُ`, `بَل رَّفَعَهُ` is `بَ ررَفَعَهُ`), so a flag on them names the letter the reference holds, not the one the spelling shows.

- **Cannot:** whether a professional reciter actually pronounced every letter (the QUD recitation is presumed correct, nobody listened); the check windows the engine graded differ from the committed transcript, so *grading* is read from the transcript the app commits, not from the window that set the kept grade, and by the truth-site scorer's carrier rule, so a decode that holds the letter next to an inserted one, or one half of a geminate the scorer split, can read as holding it (the grading list is for the owner to check); device sessions' pauses are unknown.
- QUD's intervals are clustered by mushaf and surah (two reciters: they describe these recordings, not reciters); the device logs' by log; the owner's sessions, the word sample and the held-out verified sites (selected at target-pair swaps, so their rates are not population rates) carry counts and points only.
