# The tlog consonant round's frozen look readings (#227)

Generated by `python -m training.consonant_readout freeze --spec consonant2 --half look` and `python -m training.consonant_readout doc --spec consonant2` from `tools/tadabur/consonant2_readout/freeze.json`; do not edit by hand.

**The tlog look gating set only.** No confirm row was read: this record holds the look gating rows' grades, and the confirm tranches stay sealed until the pre-claim gates pass. It is the first grade of any round-2 tlog decode.

A **false flag** is a word the app shows as not correct on a clip of presumed-correct recitation. tlog gating keeps real mistakes, so a flag here is presumed false; the catch-loss audit is what measures that. A word may carry more than one cause.

The causes are `training.word_flags`'s: `tashkeel` (a haraka mark the mushaf prescribes is missing or wrong); `soft_pair` (a consonant heard as its soft-pair partner; Muraja's soft-pair allowance forgives these); `target_pair` (a consonant heard as one of the target pairs (ذ↔ظ, س↔ش, ط↔ق); the app does not forgive these); `other_consonant` (any other consonant substitution, deletion included); `shaddah` (a doubled consonant collapsed into one); `skipped` (the app's skip marking passed the word over); `unrecorded` (the kept grade names no error, so the cause is unknown).
