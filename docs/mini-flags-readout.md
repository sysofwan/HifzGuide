## What this does not show

- It is not a gate, a bar or a decision: no threshold is read and nothing here re-decides round 3.
- One decode per model: the intervals cover the graded population's sampling, not decode-seed or quantisation variance.
- The published mini records no training checkpoint of its own, so its row is not a recipe comparison; it is the artifact the app ships.
- Fewer flags are fewer *false* flags only together with a catch-loss read, which this measurement did not run.
