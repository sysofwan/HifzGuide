Generated by `python -m training.nonpro_eval doc` from `tools/tadabur/nonpro_eval/report.json` and `overlap.json`. Evaluation only, never a training signal; nothing here sets a threshold. ## Overlap (a cheap check)

These are app-user and amateur recordings, presumed independent of Tadabur, Muaalem and EveryAyah. The check is a sample (`tadabur.nonpro_overlap`): a few parquet row groups per source drawn by a salted hash, up to ten decoded clips in each, each fingerprinted against the same ayah in every EveryAyah reciter folder (59), at #144's line (BER ≤ 0.238, fixed from #144's controls, not retuned).

**Zero matches means no overlap detected in the sample, not proven absence.** Tadabur is not read: its rows of one ayah are spread over 385 shards (#144's screen read every shard, 8 h), and its 618 reciters, like Muaalem's 27 moshafs, are named professionals; EveryAyah stands in for a published professional recording. Every decoded clip, and every qdat_bench clip, is registered in the exposure registry as the evaluation-only use `nonpro.evaluation_only` (`tools/tadabur/exposure/nonpro.evaluation_only.clips.jsonl`), which `check_disjoint` refuses to certify anything with.

## What this can and cannot say

- **Can:** how often each model commits a letter other than the mushaf's, at every target-pair carrier, in-word geminate and consonant, on recitation by app users and amateurs neither model heard; how many words the app flags there; and, on qdat_bench, how each decode agrees with expert transcripts letter by letter.
- **Cannot:** whether a disagreement or a flag is a model error or the reciter's: nobody has listened, and non-professionals do slip, so every disagreement is `pending` and every flag is a flag on presumed-correct recitation, not a measured false flag. qdat_bench's experts are the one exception, on 159 clips of one passage. Tarteel v1 keeps only clips two ASR models transcribe well, which leans towards easier clips. Its users are unnamed, so its intervals are clustered by surah.
- **Overlap:** no sampled clip matched an EveryAyah recording; Tadabur was not read (see the overlap section).
- Nothing here trains, sets a threshold, a margin or a floor, or touches the sealed panel.
