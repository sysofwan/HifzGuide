## Reading of the pre-registration

`docs/tlog-holdout-preregistration.md` ("Groups and roles", items 2-3) is applied as written, with three readings. The kNN graph is the undirected union of each row's 20 nearest by cosine: exactly 20 per row, ordered by cosine and, on an exact tie, by ascending clip id. A cutoff tie widens only the candidate set, never the graph, and the blocked search is not authoritative — discovery keeps every column within 1e-9 of the 20th blocked score and rescoring takes the whole row when that set outgrows a column block; every kept candidate's float64 cosine is then recomputed once on the CPU before the 20 are chosen and before an edge is tested against τ, so block shape and backend cannot move an edge. The 1% cap re-clusters an oversized component inside itself at τ + 0.02, then +0.02 a round, until no component exceeds the cap; the threshold stops at 1.0, above which no cosine edge survives. The τ pick, the chaining and text-independence tests and the recall bar are read from the components **before** the cap (architect decision 2026-10-24); the post-cap numbers are reported beside them for information.
