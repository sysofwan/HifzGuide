## Reading the rules

- `not_graded`: the engine kept no row for the planted word. It is reported apart and counted as neither caught nor missed.
- `protected`: a haraka drop on و ا ء ي is labelled protected (the app's exemption); every other letter is `other`.
- `copied` / `following`: an added word is a copy of its neighbour inserted right after it. The harness reports the ayah's own phoneme words, so the copy has no word number of its own: caught means a non-correct grade on the copied word or the word after it, and the two are reported apart.
- `speech span`: the pace is the median Characters per second from a clip's first word start to its last word end, recomputed by this run.
- Every word the engine does not grade correct under a perfect decode is a finding: no model and no ear is involved. Only those findings are pooled into patterns; a planted mistake the engine misses is catch capacity, read from the catch rates.
